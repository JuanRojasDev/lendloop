# LendLoop

Fictional product built as a take-home assignment for Fluence International.

## Purpose

Neighbors list tools they are willing to lend, request to borrow from each other, and get automated notifications and reminders.

## Users

- Neighbors on a phone or a laptop, in short visits, usually at home. They want to know whether a tool is free and to request it in under a minute.
- A neighborhood admin who removes listings, reviews loans and marks trusted lenders.
- Evaluators reading the code and clicking through the app. They judge structure, security thinking, UX and practicality.

## Register

Product UI. Operate mode: scanability and task completion come first. The brief asks for a "nice, minimal layout".

## Principles

- Show availability first. It is the deciding fact on every screen.
- Share as little personal data as possible: first names only, no owner details in public.
- Automation should be visible: each loan shows what was sent and when, and failures never hide.
